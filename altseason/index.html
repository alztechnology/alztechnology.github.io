    How to read it

    The indicators, in plain words

    Altcoin Season Index (breadth)

    Of the top 50 alts, how many beat Bitcoin over the period. Under 25 means Bitcoin is leading almost everything. Over 75 means most alts are leading: a broad alt season. The 30d and 7d versions show whether the regime is starting to turn before the 90d score confirms it.

    Bitcoin dominance (BTC.D)

    Bitcoin's share of the whole market. Rotations into alts usually show up as BTC.D rolling over. A falling BTC.D together with a rising total market cap is the healthier version; BTC.D falling because everything is dumping is not.

    ETH/BTC (the leading tell)

    Money often rotates from BTC into ETH first, then into large caps, then into smaller coins. ETH/BTC making higher highs is usually an early sign. If ETH/BTC keeps falling, broad alt strength tends not to last.

    TOTAL2, TOTAL3 and OTHERS.D

    TOTAL2 is the market without Bitcoin, TOTAL3 without BTC and ETH. OTHERS.D is the share of coins outside the top 10. These breaking higher shows the move is spreading from the majors to the wider market, which tends to come later in the cycle.

    Volume (confirmation)

    Price moves without volume fade easily. When alt volume grows faster than Bitcoin volume, money is actually rotating, not just thin markets drifting up.

    Stablecoin dominance and sentiment

    A rising stablecoin share means money is sitting on the sidelines (risk-off). Fear & Greed at extreme greed has often lined up with tops, and extreme fear with better entries for patient buyers. Use it as context, not a timer.

    Method. Top 50 coins by market cap from CoinGecko, excluding Bitcoin, stablecoins (USDT, USDC, DAI, USDe, FDUSD, USDS and similar), wrapped or staked tokens (WBTC, WETH, stETH, WBETH, weETH, cbBTC and similar) and tokenised funds or gold. A coin counts if its price change beat Bitcoin's over the period. Index = coins that beat BTC ÷ eligible coins × 100. 7d and 30d use CoinGecko's live percentages; 90d compares today's price with the daily close 90 days ago. The history chart uses today's top 50, so it carries some survivorship bias. Dominance and market cap come from CoinGecko's global data, which can differ slightly from TradingView's CRYPTOCAP figures.

    Sources. CoinGecko (coingecko.com) for prices, market cap, dominance and volume · TradingView (tradingview.com) for the history charts · Alternative.me (alternative.me) for the Fear & Greed Index. Index method in the spirit of Blockchain Center's Altcoin Season Index; our numbers are computed independently and may differ.
