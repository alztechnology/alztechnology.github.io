03 · How I use AI

How I use AI to draft specifications

AI drafts and organises. I review every output against the source material, confirm it with stakeholders, and only then is it used. I do not hand an unreviewed draft to an engineer or an AI agent.

Independent workflow
Independent workflow

From a business problem to testable stories

This is the method I use when I need a structured BRD, a set of user stories with acceptance criteria, or UAT cases. The point is a specification precise enough for an engineer, a reviewer, or an AI agent to implement, and for a person to verify.

InputDraft output I then review
Business problemStructured problem statement and scope
Stakeholder needsClassified requirements and a BRD draft
Current processAS-IS / TO-BE maps and a gap analysis
Agreed business rulesUser stories and testable acceptance criteria, ready for Jira or Markdown
Functional requirementsUAT scenarios and test cases
Test findingsJira-ready defects with steps, expected versus actual, and severity
Independent workflow

What stays with me

  1. Source first. I start from stakeholder notes, existing process, and the systems in scope, not from a blank prompt.
  2. Draft, then check. AI helps organise and word the draft. I check every rule, name and edge case against the source.
  3. People confirm. Business owners sign off the meaning; I do not treat a generated story as agreed.
  4. Testable wording. Acceptance criteria have to be specific enough to pass or fail in UAT.

I also use the same review-first pattern for operational tracking and structured research notes. No personal or employer data is shown on this page.
